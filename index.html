03

Unit space — speed × range × fair-fight power

Range decides who shoots first, speed who can refuse, power (hp/metal × dps/metal) who wins once neither can avoid the other. Power always uses sustained dps — Deathmark fires one 500-damage shot per 7 s (ammo_per_shot 7 · ammo_demand 1), so it is 71 dps, not 5000. Speed is always the base value; combat-speed modifiers are not applied. Range is always the anti-land weapon — secondary torpedoes and orbital weapons are excluded, so Drifter charts at 67.5 rather than its 120 torpedo. 04

Rules established

Check every proposal against these before costing it.

Range ceiling 120

non-artillery units

Towers in both factions move to 120. MLA's T2 tower is already 120 → ~140. New violators: Monstrosity 140, Scorpion 130.

Raid response

speed and range together

To be able to respond to Dox / Booms, land units need at least 14 speed and comparable range to Dox — Spark, Purger, Predator, Stryker.

T2 must unlock a manoeuvre

not scale up

Siege, hot-drop, hold ground, revive, advance under fire, counter-battery. Leveler is the only T2 unit that fails this — it's a bigger Ant.

Mirrored splash placement

the faction structure

MLA puts splash on bots and pure alpha on tanks. Legion does the reverse. Splash is anti-body, alpha is anti-armour — so each line is aimed at the opposite chassis.

05

Still open

Corsair

unassigned

Only unit in either T1 vehicle line without a verb. Its stats existed to counter Slammer's kite (§5) — which the rework removes, so it's free to redesign.

Naval roster

not extracted

Blocks Drifter — no ship HP to check its alpha against. Same gap that bit us on Hornet.

Sheller vs bubble shields

decision, not a number

Earthshaker regenerates 300/s; Sheller does 146 dps per 1000 metal. Should bubble-breaking require ignore_shields, or should massed artillery work? If the latter, the lever is the regen, not the Sheller.

Bluehawk

role unsettled

Currently the only T2 unit that can shoot through shields. Previously caused problems with kiting.

Ideas:
· Long range, durable and fast AA, no anti-ground?
· Long range non-homing missiles, anti-static and anti-ship? Role/range swap with Sheller?
· Mid-short range, forces engagements?
· Slow / stationary when firing?

Scorpion

role unclear

Possibly a siege tank with very poor mobility and manoeuvrability while in combat? Locks in, but needs shields and Havocs to guard it from being flanked or rushed.
