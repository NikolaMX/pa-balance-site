04

Rules established

Constraints applied to every proposal.

Range ceiling 120

non-artillery units

Towers in both factions move to 120. MLA's T2 tower is already 120 → ~140. New violators: Monstrosity 140, Scorpion 130.

Raid response

speed and range together

Responding to Dox / Boom requires speed ≥ 14 and range comparable to Dox — Spark, Purger, Predator, Stryker.

T2 must unlock a manoeuvre

not scale up

Siege, zone ground, hold ground, revive, advance under fire, counter-battery. Leveler is the only T2 unit that fails this.

Mirrored splash placement

the faction structure

MLA: splash on bots, pure alpha on tanks. Legion: the reverse. Splash is anti-body, alpha is anti-armour. One exception: Predator, splash removed.

04

Rules established

Constraints applied to every air and AA proposal.

Three orthogonal AA axes

what an AA unit is for

AoE pays against clumping. Targeting — seeking missiles overkill, flak and beams do not. Guidance — ballistic is anti-slow and whiffs fast movers; seeking and beam are anti-fast.

Missiles cannot retarget mid-flight

engine limit

Every salvo already in the air is wasted on a kill — roughly dps × travel time. Velocity is the efficiency lever, not the damage/rof split. Flak sidesteps this: the burst lands on the clump whether or not the aimed unit died.

T2 is diagonal, not a flat upgrade

each tier owns a target class

Each target type gets exactly one air answer, and no role overlap. Fighters are slightly more durable than bombers so dogfights last, while AA damage still attritions fighters.

Air control snowballs

so AA is a brake, not a wall

Ground AA has to be a persistent attrition cost rather than annihilation. Bombers cannot shoot air, which is what makes interceptor fragility affordable.

05

Still open

Legion air and AA

no role pass yet

Ten aircraft and six AA units carry shipped numbers and no assigned role. Every MLA role below has a Legion counterpart that has not been checked against it.

Hornet standoff

resolved — Gil-E keeps AA

Gil-E is the only ground counter to a standoff Hornet — r200 against Hornet's r190, one-shotting its 600 hp. The land pass briefly removed Air from its target layers; that is reverted and Gil-E keeps AA. Open part: nothing else on the ground reaches r190.

Flak sustained dps

number pass outstanding

Storm and Flak Cannon need roughly 2× sustained AoE dps to grind 250–300 hp Kestrels. Storm's mod build is at 6 × 100 per carpet; the Cannon has not been touched.

Fire-from-transport

mechanic unspecified

Pelican carries one unit that fires from inside. Firing arc, drop window and the combat-speed trigger are all unspecified.
